    Museum of Ordinary Afternoons

    A museum of moments in which nothing happens: a cup of tea going cold, a cat watching a door, 3:40 pm on a Tuesday. Visitors come to be underwhelmed, precisely.

    Objects arrive at the receiving desk. Choose a gallery, then write the wall label from the fragments offered. Every gallery advertises how uneventful it is on the Ordinary Afternoon Scale (OAS), where 0 is nothing at all and 10 is a wasp getting in. Hang each object so its label reads within 0.75 of what the gallery promises.

    Three incidents (a label more than 1.75 too exciting) and the Council closes you. Don't keep the courier waiting, or Gerald from Security will hang it himself. Mouse, touch, or keys: 1–4 gallery, Q W E title, A S D description, Z X C note, Enter to hang.
