      Communiqué No. 1For immediate lunch

      Sandwich Diplomacy

      Ingredients swing over the sandwich one at a time. Drop each one on top. Every ingredient has a feud or an alliance, and it judges whoever it touches. Feuds raise tension, alliances ease it, and a lopsided stack raises it too.

      Reach 14 layers before the tension tops out and the sandwich becomes a salad. Hold one ingredient aside, or veto three outright. The rulebook will be amended. You will be told.
