    Welcome to the slowest motor race in the calendar.

    Very Slow Grand Prix

    Three laps. Five competitors. The winner is whoever finishes last.

    • Every rival must cross the line before you do.
    • Never stop. Below 0.25 m/s for 1.5 seconds and the stewards disqualify you for loitering.
    • Your engine gets bored below 0.9 m/s. When boredom is full, the car lurches forward on its own.
    • Lead the race for too long and you are declared the winner. The shame gauge ends your career.
    • Expect track works on lap two and a restless finish line on lap three.
    Hold the throttle button, the track, Space or Up arrow. Release to coast.
