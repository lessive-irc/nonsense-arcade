    The deck draws you

    You sit down at the kitchen table. The deck shuffles itself, and the cards you got through today come back, face down. They would like to read your day to you. Turn them over.

    A deck of 19 inconveniences and 16 small graces

    Get through one ordinary day

    Morning, commute, work. Each inconvenience is turned over in turn and will cost you composure, punctuality or dignity when you endure it. You have three spoons per inconvenience to spend on cards from your hand: gain a stat back, block a loss, or cancel an inconvenience of the matching suit. Major Arcana cannot be cancelled.

    If any stat reaches zero, the day is over. Between stages you add one card to your deck. Then it is evening.
