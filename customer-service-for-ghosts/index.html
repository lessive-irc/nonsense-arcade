ON HOLD
    Night Exchange · Position vacant · Hours 23:00–05:00

    Customer Service for Ghosts

    The departed ring in at night. They want the decade they're calling about, and the right department within it. You have a headset, a board and a lot of cord.

    Read each caller's complaint for clues to their era: gas lamps, rationing, a modem. Plug them into Grievance for complaints, Missing for lost property, or Linger for unfinished business. Callers left waiting go spectral and hang up. Five dropped calls and you're let go.

    Drag a cord from a caller to a jack, or tap the caller and then the jack. Keyboard: 1–4 pick a line, arrows and Enter plug it, H for “please hold”.

    The HOLD lamp is lit

    The board goes quiet. Every lamp is dark except one you have never seen lit: the painted-over jack in the corner marked HOLD.

    The strings stop. A voice says, “Hello? Is that the operator?”

    It is your voice. It has been on hold since March 1987. It has been playing to every caller tonight, and several of them complimented it.

    Calls between decades must be relayed through each decade in between (Exchange Regulation 14b). Route yourself through, one decade at a time. Don't put yourself on hold. You know how that goes.
