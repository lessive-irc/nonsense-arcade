A note for the maestro

  • Turn the dial with ↑ ↓, the − + buttons, or by dragging the knob. The stew takes about two seconds to answer, like every great soprano.
  • The score flows in from the right. Keep the stew's voice inside the gold band. A simmer is 85 to 95 °C.
  • At 100 °C it shrieks and the broth boils away. Below 55 °C the fat sets and it mumbles into aspic.
  • When an ingredient reaches the stew, press Space or tap Add. Cold ingredients take a few degrees with them.
