    Between Mount Gorrow, stratovolcano, 2,340 m (“the Volcano”), and the undersigned (“the Intern”). Three weeks, then a performance review.

    1. 1.1The Intern shall receive no remuneration, monetary, geothermal or otherwise.
    2. 4.1Hours shall be recorded to the nearest half hour and paid to the nearest £0.00.
    3. 7(b)An eruption request not answered by 09:00 on its requested date is deemed approved.

    6. 11The Intern may take lunch from 12:00 to 12:20, provided nothing is erupting.
