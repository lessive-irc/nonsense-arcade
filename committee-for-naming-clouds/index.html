The Committee for Naming Clouds 214th meeting · Upper Room, Bramley Library

Clouds do not wait for committees. This committee has never once accepted that.

You are in the Chair. A cloud drifts across the window; the members propose names for it. Pick a motion, wait for a seconder, put the question, and bang the gavel while the hands are still up, before the cloud evaporates.

  1. A good name says what the cloud is. Dr Halloran objects to the wrong genus.
  2. The quorum is four. Members wander off and go off topic: click them (or press A–H) to bring them back.
  3. Nothing counts unless Ms Okonkwo is in the room to minute it.
  4. Three clouds lost, or a long spell inquorate, and the committee is dissolved.
