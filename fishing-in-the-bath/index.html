Fishing in the Bath

The bath is deeper than it should be. Things live in it. Catch them, catalogue them, and try not to think about how far down it goes.

Hold to charge a cast and release to throw. When the float dips, press to strike. Then hold to raise the green box and keep it over the catch until the line is full.

Bait

Hold Space, the mouse or a finger on the bath. 1–4 change bait.
